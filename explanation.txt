What its actually focus on or in other words what is goal
so see knee have following abnormalities 
1. Abnormal
2. ACL Tear (Anterior Cruciate Ligament tear)
3. Meniscus Tear

So the goal is to predict whether the knee is abnormal , ACL tear , or Meniscus tear 

So the mathematics we are using is 
Single multi-task model: three sigmoid outputs per exam — P(abnormal), P(ACL tear), P(meniscal tear).

Per-plane models: for each plane (axial/coronal/sagittal), three outputs per exam; you then aggregate across planes (average, max, or learned fusion) to get final exam-level probabilities.

Thresholded decisions: convert probabilities to 0/1 using a chosen threshold, e.g. P(ACL tear) > 0.5 → ACL tear predicted.
